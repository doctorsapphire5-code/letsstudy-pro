Acceptable Use Policy

Guidelines for responsible, lawful and respectful use of the LetsStudy Pro Global Digital Learning & Growth Ecosystem.

Last updated: November 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy explains how individuals, professionals, educators, creators, businesses, organizations and other users may access and use LetsStudy Pro.

By accessing or using our website, services, accounts or digital features, you agree to follow this policy and our applicable Terms and Conditions.

1. Purpose of This Policy

LetsStudy Pro provides an integrated digital ecosystem designed to connect people, knowledge, technology, businesses and opportunities.

This policy helps protect the platform, its users, partners and content while promoting a safe, professional and productive digital environment.

2. Lawful Use

You must use LetsStudy Pro only for lawful purposes. You must not:

  • Violate applicable local, national or international laws.
  • Use the platform to facilitate fraud, scams or criminal activities.
  • Promote violence, exploitation or illegal discrimination.
  • Publish content that unlawfully violates another person's rights.
  • Use the platform to threaten, harass or intimidate others.

3. Account Responsibility

Users are responsible for maintaining the security and accuracy of their accounts.

  • Provide accurate and truthful registration information.
  • Keep your login credentials confidential.
  • Do not impersonate another individual or organization.
  • Do not access another user's account without authorization.
  • Notify us if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.
  • Do not create accounts for deceptive or abusive purposes.

4. Content Standards

Users who submit, publish, upload or share content must ensure that it is appropriate, accurate and lawful.

You must not submit or distribute:

  • False, misleading or deliberately deceptive information.
  • Malware, viruses or malicious software.
  • Spam, unsolicited promotional messages or repetitive content.
  • Content that infringes copyright, trademarks or other intellectual property rights.
  • Private or confidential information without proper authorization.
  • Fraudulent job offers, fake scholarships or deceptive business listings.
  • Abusive, threatening or unlawfully harmful material.

5. Learning and Educational Activities

Users may access and share educational materials, courses, research, learning resources and other knowledge-based content.

  • Respect academic integrity and intellectual property.
  • Do not submit plagiarized work as your own.
  • Do not distribute paid or protected materials without permission.
  • Do not misrepresent qualifications, certificates or academic achievements.
  • Use educational tools responsibly and honestly.

6. Careers, Scholarships and Opportunities

Users and organizations participating in careers, recruitment, scholarships and opportunity services must:

  • Provide truthful descriptions and eligibility requirements.
  • Not publish fake employment or scholarship opportunities.
  • Not request money through deceptive recruitment practices.
  • Respect applicant privacy and personal information.
  • Not discriminate unlawfully against applicants.

Users should independently verify opportunities and application requirements before submitting personal information or making payments to third parties.

7. Business, Marketplace and Services

Businesses, service providers, freelancers, sellers and organizations must use the ecosystem honestly and professionally.

  • Provide accurate business and service information.
  • Describe products and services truthfully.
  • Do not advertise prohibited or illegal products and services.
  • Do not manipulate reviews, ratings or marketplace activity.
  • Do not engage in fraudulent transactions or deceptive pricing.
  • Respect customer rights and applicable consumer protection laws.

8. Artificial Intelligence and Technology

AI-powered tools and technology features must be used responsibly and in accordance with applicable laws.

  • Do not use AI tools to generate scams or malicious content.
  • Do not use the platform to develop or distribute malware.
  • Do not use automated tools to abuse or overload our services.
  • Review AI-generated information before relying on it.
  • Do not present AI-generated information as verified facts when it has not been checked.
  • Respect privacy, consent and intellectual property rights.

9. Platform Security

You must not attempt to compromise the security, availability or integrity of LetsStudy Pro.

  • Do not attempt unauthorized access to systems or accounts.
  • Do not exploit security vulnerabilities without authorization.
  • Do not interfere with platform operations or infrastructure.
  • Do not bypass access controls or account permissions.
  • Do not scrape or collect personal data unlawfully.
  • Do not conduct denial-of-service attacks or abusive automated requests.

10. Privacy and Personal Information

Users must respect the privacy and personal information of other individuals and organizations.

  • Do not publish another person's sensitive information without authorization.
  • Do not misuse contact details or account information.
  • Do not collect personal data through deceptive methods.
  • Use information obtained through the platform only for legitimate purposes.

Our handling of personal information is explained in our Privacy Policy.

11. Advertising and Promotions

Advertisers, partners and users promoting products, services or opportunities must ensure that promotional material is truthful, lawful and not misleading.

  • Do not publish deceptive advertisements.
  • Do not use misleading claims or fake endorsements.
  • Do not distribute spam or unauthorized promotional campaigns.
  • Clearly disclose relevant commercial relationships where required.

12. Reporting Violations

Users are encouraged to report content or activities that appear to violate this policy.

To report an issue, visit our Report Content page or contact our support team.

Reports should be submitted honestly and with sufficient information to help us review the issue.

13. Enforcement and Actions

Where appropriate, LetsStudy Pro may investigate suspected violations and take reasonable action, subject to applicable law and our other policies.

Possible actions may include:

  • Removing or restricting access to violating content.
  • Issuing warnings or requesting corrective action.
  • Restricting specific platform features.
  • Suspending or terminating accounts where justified.
  • Preserving relevant information or reporting matters to appropriate authorities when legally required or permitted.

Actions will depend on the nature, seriousness and context of the violation. Where appropriate, users may contact support to request a review.

14. Third-Party Services

LetsStudy Pro may provide links to external websites, applications, services or opportunities. Those services may operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

We are not responsible for independent third-party content, services or practices except where applicable law provides otherwise.

15. Policy Updates

We may update this Acceptable Use Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, security requirements or legal obligations.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date. Continued use after an update takes effect is subject to the updated policy.
