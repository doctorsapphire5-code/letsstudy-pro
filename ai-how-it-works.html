Responsible AI

AI is a powerful assistant, but responsible usage and human judgment remain important.

  • AI-generated information may contain errors.
  • Verify important facts using reliable sources.
  • Do not submit passwords or confidential information.
  • Test generated code before deploying it.
  • Review AI-generated professional and research documents.
  • AI recommendations do not guarantee specific outcomes.
