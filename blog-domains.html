🚀 How to Connect

  1. Add your domain above.
  2. Copy the DNS records shown by LetsStudy Pro.
  3. Add those records at your domain provider.
  4. Wait for DNS propagation.
  5. Click Verify.
  6. After verification, SSL/HTTPS can be activated.
