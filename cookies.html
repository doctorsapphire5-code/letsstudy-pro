Cookies & Similar Technologies

This Cookies Policy explains how LetsStudy Pro uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use our digital platform.

Cookies can help us remember preferences, maintain essential functionality, improve security, understand how our platform is used, and improve the overall user experience.

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time using the controls below.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files stored on your device by a website. They allow a website to remember information about your visit and can support functions such as preferences, authentication, security and analytics.

2. Types of Cookies We May Use

Essential Cookies

These may be necessary for core website functions, security, navigation, authentication or remembering essential settings.

Preference Cookies

These may remember choices such as language, interface preferences or other settings so that your experience can be more convenient.

Analytics Cookies

These may help us understand how visitors use LetsStudy Pro, including which pages are visited and how users interact with the platform.

Advertising Cookies

Where advertising services are enabled, advertising technologies may use cookies or similar technologies to measure advertising performance or provide relevant advertisements, subject to applicable settings and requirements.

3. Why We Use Cookies

  • To keep essential platform functions working.
  • To improve website performance and usability.
  • To remember selected preferences.
  • To support account and security functions.
  • To understand platform usage and improve our services.
  • To measure content and advertising performance where applicable.

4. Third-Party Technologies

Some LetsStudy Pro features may use third-party technologies, such as analytics, authentication, video, advertising or other service providers.

These providers may place or access their own cookies or similar technologies according to their respective policies and configurations.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. You may also disable or delete cookies already stored on your device.

Blocking essential cookies may affect certain functionality of the LetsStudy Pro platform.
