LetsStudy Pro may allow creators, educators, businesses and users to submit courses, resources, videos, articles, images, documents, services and other content.

The person or organization that owns the relevant rights generally remains responsible for those rights.

By submitting content, you represent that you have the necessary rights, permissions or authorization to submit and share it.

3. Prohibited Activities

Users should not use LetsStudy Pro to:

  • Upload pirated courses, books, videos or software.
  • Copy another creator's work and claim it as their own.
  • Distribute copyrighted material without authorization.
  • Remove copyright or ownership notices.
  • Upload unauthorized recordings of paid content.
  • Sell protected materials without permission.
  • Use copyrighted images, music or videos without appropriate rights.
  • Knowingly submit infringing content.

4. Copyright Infringement Reports

If you believe that content available through LetsStudy Pro infringes your copyright, you may contact us with enough information for us to investigate.

A report should generally include:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work.
  • Description of the allegedly infringing material.
  • The URL or location where the material appears.
  • Information supporting your ownership or authority.
  • An explanation of why the use is unauthorized.
  • A statement that the information provided is accurate.

5. Review & Removal

Copyright complaints may be reviewed based on the information provided, the applicable law and the circumstances of the case.

Where appropriate, LetsStudy Pro may restrict, remove or disable access to content that appears to infringe applicable rights.

We may request additional information from the reporting party or the content contributor when necessary.

6. Counter-Notice

If your content has been restricted or removed because of a copyright complaint and you believe the action was incorrect, you may contact us with relevant information explaining your position.

Any dispute or counter-notice process will be handled according to applicable law and the circumstances of the matter.

7. Repeat Infringement

Accounts or contributors that repeatedly submit material that infringes copyright or other intellectual property rights may have their content, accounts or platform access restricted or terminated where appropriate.
