Verify a certificate

Enter the certificate ID printed on the certificate, or open the link from its QR code or verification line. We’ll tell you whether it is genuine.

The ID looks like LSP-2026-0001. Spaces at the start or end are ignored.

What this check covers

We compare the ID you enter with the certificates LetsStudy Pro has issued. A result of “Genuine” means the certificate is on record and in good standing. If the name or course on your copy differs from what is shown here, treat the copy as altered and contact us.
