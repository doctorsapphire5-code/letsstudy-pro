Creator Guidelines

Build valuable content, share your expertise and help people Learn. Build. Work. Grow.

Welcome, Creators

LetsStudy Pro is a global digital learning and growth ecosystem where creators can share knowledge, skills, resources, services and practical experiences.

These guidelines help creators produce useful, trustworthy, original and professional content for the LetsStudy Pro community.

Creator principle: Create content that provides genuine value to the people who use LetsStudy Pro.

1. Who Can Become a Creator?

Creators may include:

  • Teachers and educators
  • Students and learners
  • Freelancers
  • Developers and technology professionals
  • Designers and video editors
  • Writers and researchers
  • Business professionals
  • Career and skills experts
  • Content creators
  • Organizations and other qualified contributors

2. Types of Content

Depending on your role and access, you may contribute content such as:

  • Online courses
  • Lessons and educational materials
  • Study notes
  • Past papers and learning resources
  • Educational articles
  • Videos
  • Guides and tutorials
  • Digital skills content
  • Professional services
  • Career resources
  • Business knowledge
  • Other useful digital resources

3. Create Original Content

Whenever possible, publish content that you created yourself or have permission to distribute.

Good practice: Add your own explanations, examples, exercises, demonstrations and practical insights.

Do not copy another person's work and present it as your own.

See our Copyright Policy for more information.

4. Educational Quality

Educational content should be clear, useful and appropriate for the intended audience.

  • Use clear titles.
  • Explain concepts accurately.
  • Organize lessons logically.
  • Use practical examples where appropriate.
  • Identify the expected learning outcome.
  • Avoid deliberately misleading information.
  • Update content when important information becomes outdated.

5. Course & Lesson Guidelines

If you create a course, structure it so learners can follow the material progressively.

  • Start with an appropriate introduction.
  • Organize lessons into meaningful modules.
  • Use descriptive lesson titles.
  • Keep individual lessons focused.
  • Include practical activities where useful.
  • Clearly describe the course level.
  • State prerequisites when necessary.

6. Video Guidelines

Video creators should aim for content that is understandable and professionally presented.

  • Use a clear title and description.
  • Use an appropriate thumbnail.
  • Ensure the video is relevant to its title.
  • Use understandable audio where applicable.
  • Avoid unnecessary copyrighted material.
  • Provide useful explanations rather than misleading clickbait.

7. Resources & Documents

When publishing notes, documents, study materials or other resources, make sure you have the right to distribute them.

  • Clearly identify the resource.
  • Use accurate descriptions.
  • Credit sources when appropriate.
  • Do not upload pirated materials.
  • Do not distribute restricted materials without authorization.

8. Freelance Services

Creators offering professional services should provide accurate information about what they can deliver.

  • Describe your service clearly.
  • List relevant skills.
  • Provide realistic pricing where applicable.
  • State delivery expectations clearly.
  • Do not make false professional claims.
  • Communicate professionally with clients.

9. Professional Conduct

Creators are expected to communicate respectfully with learners, clients, businesses and other members of the ecosystem.

  • Be respectful.
  • Respond professionally.
  • Do not harass other users.
  • Do not impersonate another person or organization.
  • Do not manipulate reviews or ratings.
  • Do not use deceptive practices.

10. Prohibited Content

Creators should not submit content that is illegal, fraudulent, intentionally deceptive, abusive, infringing or otherwise inappropriate for the platform.

Content that violates applicable laws, intellectual property rights, platform rules or the rights of others may be restricted or removed.

11. Accuracy & Transparency

Creators should be transparent about their qualifications, experience, affiliations and the nature of their content when those details are relevant.

Do not fabricate qualifications, certificates, achievements, employment history or professional experience.

12. AI-Assisted Content

Creators may use appropriate digital and AI tools to assist with research, drafting, editing, design or production, where permitted.

Creators remain responsible for reviewing their published content for accuracy, originality, quality and applicable rights.

13. Privacy & Personal Information

Do not publish another person's private or sensitive information without an appropriate lawful basis or permission.

Take reasonable care when creating content that includes names, photographs, contact information or other personal information.

14. Content Review

Submitted creator profiles and content may be reviewed before or after publication depending on the relevant LetsStudy Pro feature.

Review may consider content quality, accuracy, copyright, safety, authenticity and compliance with applicable platform rules.

15. Content Changes or Removal

LetsStudy Pro may restrict, edit, unpublish or remove content when appropriate under applicable rules, agreements or law.

Creators may be contacted when additional information or clarification is needed.

16. Creator Responsibilities

  • Keep your profile information accurate.
  • Protect your account credentials.
  • Respect copyright and intellectual property.
  • Respect other creators and users.
  • Provide truthful information.
  • Maintain the quality of published content.
  • Follow applicable laws and platform policies.

17. Before You Publish

Ask yourself:

  • Is this content useful?
  • Did I create it or have permission to use it?
  • Is the information reasonably accurate?
  • Is the title an accurate representation?
  • Would a learner or client understand what they are receiving?
  • Does the content respect other people's rights?
