Frequently Asked Questions

Open the course page and select the Enroll button. If the course is paid, complete checkout first. After successful enrollment, the course will appear in your learning dashboard.
Go to your dashboard and open My Courses. Select the course you want to continue. Your learning progress is saved to your account.
Open the Resources or Notes & Papers section. Select the resource you want and use the download option when available.
Open the login page and select Forgot Password. Enter your registered email address and follow the password reset instructions sent to your email.
After registration, check your email for the verification message from LetsStudy Pro. Open it and follow the verification link.
Open your Profile and select Edit Profile. You can update the information that your account allows you to change.
Select the course, continue to checkout and choose an available payment method. Follow the payment instructions shown on the checkout page.
Check your payment details and try again. If money was deducted but your order was not completed, keep your transaction information and contact support.
A pending payment may require additional processing time. Check your order status before making another payment to avoid duplicate transactions.
Complete the required course requirements. When eligible, your certificate will become available through your certificate section.
Use the Certificate Verification page and enter the certificate ID or scan the verification QR code when one is provided.
You can earn learning points through activities such as completing courses, quizzes and learning milestones. Eligible activities can also unlock badges and achievements.
