Popular Questions

Some quick answers before contacting support.

How do I access my courses? +
Open your dashboard and select the course you have enrolled in. Your available learning materials and progress will appear there.
How can I verify my certificate? +
Open the certificate verification page and enter the unique Certificate ID shown on your certificate.
Where can I see my payments? +
You can review your billing information, invoices and transactions from the relevant payment and billing pages.
What should I do if a payment fails? +
Check your payment information and try again. If the issue continues, contact support and include your order or transaction information.
How do I contact support? +
Use the Contact Support page to send a support request, or create a support ticket so that your issue can be tracked.
How do I report an issue in the community? +
Use the available report tools on community posts or profiles. For account-related issues, contact support.
