How to Win an Internship

Build a Strong CV

Highlight your education, projects, skills, achievements, certifications, volunteering and practical experience. Keep your CV focused on the internship you want.

Build Real Projects

Projects can prove what you can do even when you have little professional experience. Build a portfolio that demonstrates your practical skills.

Prepare for Interviews

Research the organization, understand the internship role, practice common questions and prepare examples showing your problem-solving and teamwork abilities.

Build Your Network

Connect with professionals, mentors, alumni and organizations. Professional relationships can expose you to opportunities that are never publicly advertised.

Look Beyond Your Country

International and remote internships can help you gain experience working with diverse teams and global organizations.

Keep Learning

Use your internship to identify skill gaps and continue learning through courses, certifications, projects and professional practice.

Apply Strategically

Do not send the same application everywhere. Match your CV, cover letter and portfolio to the requirements of each internship.

Think Long-Term

The best internship is not always the one with the highest short-term pay. Consider mentorship, skills, projects, networking and future employment opportunities.

Avoid Internship Scams

Verify the organization and application channel. Be cautious when someone demands unusual payments or sensitive information in exchange for a guaranteed internship.

Turn Experience Into Your Career

An internship should help you move from learning to doing. Build relationships, ask good questions, document your work, learn from feedback and leave with practical skills and evidence of what you achieved.
