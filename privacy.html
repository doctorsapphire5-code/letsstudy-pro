Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters
LetsStudy Pro respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you use our website, applications, courses and services.

1. About LetsStudy Pro

LetsStudy Pro is an online educational platform that provides learning resources, courses, AI-powered learning tools, educational content and related digital services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use LetsStudy Pro, we may collect the following information:

2.1 Account Information

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Firebase Authentication user ID
  • Profile information you choose to provide

2.2 Learning Information

  • Courses you enroll in
  • Lessons viewed
  • Learning progress
  • Quiz results
  • Certificates
  • Course reviews and ratings

2.3 AI Usage Information

If you use LetsStudy Pro AI, we may process the questions, prompts and responses required to provide the AI service. We may also record usage information such as the number of AI requests made by your account for purposes including usage limits, security and service management.

2.4 Payment Information

When you purchase a course or membership, payment processing may be handled by third-party payment providers. LetsStudy Pro does not need to store your complete card number or card security code on our servers. Payment providers may process payment information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

2.5 Technical Information

We may automatically receive technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Referring pages
  • Approximate location information
  • Cookies and similar technologies

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide educational services
  • Provide access to purchased courses
  • Track learning progress
  • Provide AI learning assistance
  • Process transactions
  • Issue certificates
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our website and services
  • Detect fraud, abuse and security threats
  • Measure website performance
  • Understand how users interact with our services

4. Firebase Services

LetsStudy Pro may use Firebase services provided by Google for functionality such as authentication, databases, hosting, analytics and application services. Firebase may process information according to Google's applicable policies and terms.

5. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and user interaction. Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, device information, browser information and general usage patterns. This information helps us improve the performance and usability of LetsStudy Pro.

6. Advertising

LetsStudy Pro may display advertisements through third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalize advertisements where permitted by applicable settings and laws.

Users may have options to manage personalized advertising through the settings provided by the relevant advertising provider.

7. Cookies

LetsStudy Pro may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Authentication
  • Security
  • Remembering preferences
  • Analytics
  • Advertising
  • Improving website functionality

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate LetsStudy Pro, including providers supporting:

  • Authentication
  • Cloud hosting
  • Analytics
  • Payments
  • AI services
  • Security
  • Customer support

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process or to protect the rights, safety and security of LetsStudy Pro, our users or others.

10. AI Services

LetsStudy Pro AI may use third-party AI infrastructure to generate educational responses. Do not submit highly sensitive personal information, passwords, payment card details or other confidential information into an AI prompt.

11. Children's Privacy

LetsStudy Pro is an educational platform. Where applicable, users should have the permission of a parent, guardian or authorized institution when required by local law. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

12. Your Account Choices

Depending on the services available, you may be able to:

  • Update your account information
  • Change your password
  • Manage your profile
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Contact support regarding your data

13. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

14. Third-Party Links

LetsStudy Pro may contain links to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites. Users should review the privacy policies of those websites before providing personal information.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
