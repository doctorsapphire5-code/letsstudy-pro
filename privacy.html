Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LetsStudy Pro handles information when you use our website, learning platform, community and related services.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to LetsStudy Pro. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting information associated with your use of our platform.

This Privacy Policy describes the general types of information that may be collected, how that information may be used, and the choices available to users.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the services you use, LetsStudy Pro may process information such as:

  • Name and profile information.
  • Email address and account information.
  • Authentication and account security information.
  • Course enrollment and learning progress.
  • Certificates, achievements and related records.
  • Orders, subscriptions and payment-related records.
  • Community posts, comments and messages you submit.
  • Technical information such as browser, device and service usage information.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide courses and educational services.
  • Track learning progress and achievements.
  • Provide certificates and verification services.
  • Process orders and subscriptions.
  • Provide community and communication features.
  • Improve platform performance and user experience.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, security threats and unauthorized access.
  • Provide customer support.

4. Authentication

LetsStudy Pro may use Firebase Authentication or other authentication technologies to securely manage user accounts and sign-in sessions.

Users should keep their passwords and authentication credentials private and should immediately report suspected unauthorized access.

5. Payments

When you purchase a course, subscription or other paid service, payment information may be processed through third-party payment providers.

LetsStudy Pro may receive transaction information needed to confirm orders, update access and maintain billing records. We do not claim to store complete payment-card credentials unless explicitly stated by the applicable payment provider and service architecture.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The platform may use cookies, local storage, session technologies or similar mechanisms to maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve performance and support security.

Your browser may provide controls for managing cookies and related technologies.

7. Educational and Community Content

Content that you voluntarily publish through community features may become visible to other authorized users. Do not publish sensitive personal information that you do not want other people to see.

8. Third-Party Services

LetsStudy Pro may integrate third-party services such as authentication, hosting, analytics, payment, communication or educational technology providers.

Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their accounts.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, enforce agreements and protect the platform.

11. Your Choices

Depending on the service and applicable law, users may have options concerning their account information, communications and certain data processing activities.

You may contact support if you need assistance with your account or privacy-related request.

12. Children's Privacy

Users should provide information appropriate for their age and circumstances. Where applicable, additional requirements may apply to services involving children or minors.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, legal requirements or data practices change.

The latest version will be made available on this page.

14. Contact Us

For privacy or account questions, visit our Contact page or use the support channels provided by LetsStudy Pro.
