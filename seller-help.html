Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common seller questions.

Open Seller Products and select Add Product. Enter your product information, price, images and status, then publish the product.
Open Seller Orders to view customer orders. You can inspect order information and update the order status according to your fulfillment process.
Your eligible earnings can be viewed in Seller Earnings. Use Seller Payouts to submit a payout request and track its status.
Open Seller Inventory and update the available quantity for each product. Keep stock information accurate so customers see correct availability.
Seller Reviews lets you view customer feedback, ratings and review information associated with your store products or services.
Open Seller Analytics to view your store performance, sales trends and other available seller metrics.
