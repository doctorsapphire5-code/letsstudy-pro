Quick Answers

Frequently asked support questions.

How do I contact LetsStudy Pro Support? +
You can contact support through WhatsApp at +255 610 983 928, email support@letsstudy.pro, or create a support ticket.
How do I track my support request? +
Open the My Tickets page to see your existing support requests. Select a ticket to open its ticket details.
What information should I include in a ticket? +
Describe the problem clearly and include relevant information such as your order ID, transaction ID, course name, certificate ID or screenshots when appropriate. Never include passwords or private authentication codes.
Can I contact support through WhatsApp? +
Yes. Use the WhatsApp Support button to start a conversation with LetsStudy Pro Support.
