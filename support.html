Quick answers

Frequently asked support questions.

How do I contact LetsStudy Pro Support?

Message us on WhatsApp at +255 610 983 928, email support@letsstudy.pro, or create a support ticket.

How do I track my support request?

Open My Tickets to see all your requests. Select a ticket to open its details and read replies from our team.

What should I include in a ticket?

Describe the problem clearly and add details such as your order ID, transaction ID, course name, certificate ID or a screenshot. Never include passwords or private authentication codes.

Can I contact support on WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the Chat on WhatsApp button to start a conversation with LetsStudy Pro Support.

I had a problem with a payment. What should I do?

Create a ticket with your order ID, transaction ID and a screenshot of the payment confirmation, if you have one.

I can’t sign in to my account.

Use Forgot password to reset your password. If it still doesn’t work, contact us and tell us the email you registered with. Never share your password or codes.
