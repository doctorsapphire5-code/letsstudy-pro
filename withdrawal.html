How it works

1. Your earnings are recorded under your own Admin ID.

2. Any applicable platform commission is deducted when the earning is created.

3. Your remaining earnings become available in your wallet.

4. Enter the amount you want to withdraw.

5. Your withdrawal request is sent to the Admin review system.

6. Once approved and paid, the amount is removed from your available balance.
